# Reaper

Reaper finds the movies and shows nobody watches. It explains why it thinks each one is expendable and removes them safely through Sonarr and Radarr.

<Callout tone="tip">
  **It ships read-only.** A new install can scan, score, and explain, and
  nothing else, until you arm deletion with a password, or a deploy sets it on
  at first boot.
</Callout>

## Start here

- Check **[What Reaper does](/getting-started/overview)** if you're still deciding.
- **[Install](/getting-started/install)** it on Docker, Unraid, Windows, macOS, or a snap.
- Follow the **[First run](/getting-started/first-run)** guide for the right order of steps.
- Use **[Understanding policy](/policy/understanding-policy)** to tune things once it's scanning.

## Every verdict shows its work

Reaper explains your keeps and your deletes. Every candidate shows the signals that counted against it **and** every protection that was checked and didn't fire, along with the real numbers for each one.

Two independent layers sit under every deletion and each is checked on its own. The refusal lives at the network layer. A missing rating or an unreachable service can only ever protect a file.

[How a delete is kept safe](/safety/how-a-delete-is-kept-safe) walks you through the whole path.
