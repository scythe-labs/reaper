# Reaper

Reaper finds the movies and shows nobody watches, explains why it thinks each one is
expendable, and removes them safely through Sonarr and Radarr.

<Callout tone="tip">
  **It ships read-only.** A new install can scan, score, and explain, and nothing else, until
  you arm deletion with a password.
</Callout>

  tune it.

## Every verdict shows its work

Reaper explains the keeps as well as the deletes. Every candidate carries the signals that
counted against it **and** every protection that was checked and did not fire, with the real
numbers behind each one.

Two independent layers sit under every deletion, and each is checked on its own. Deletion is off
until you turn it on, the refusal lives at the network layer, and a missing rating or an
unreachable service can only ever protect a file.

[How a delete is kept safe](/safety/how-a-delete-is-kept-safe) is the page that walks it
through.
